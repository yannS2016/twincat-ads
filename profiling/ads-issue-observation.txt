Hardenning --> PLC seems to clear its previous ahandle because

A bit of discovery about ads-ioc: when the ioc host falls off the network, this causes a connection loss to the PLC, which triggers an internal reconnection attempt from the ads-ioc, which may or may not succeed. But when the ads-ioc does reconnect to the PLC, it seems the PLC treats this reconnection as a fresh one, losing the previously established handshakes it made to resolve the DB symbols against the ADS handles. causing the notorious Sum read 0 failed: status 1797 . The ads-ioc will need some hardening to recover the connection cleanly.
